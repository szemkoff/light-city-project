For investors

Patient capital. Measurable returns.

Light City is structured for serious diligence: phased capital expenditure, documented assumptions, governance milestones and a clear line between proven infrastructure and exploratory research.

Why it is investable

Ambitious in form. Conservative in engineering.

Proven infrastructure

Microgrid, mass timber and geodesic structures already stand elsewhere. 2–5 MWp of solar in Phase 1 is carried as engineering, not research.

Phased commitments

Capital is staged with each development stage, so every tranche is tied to a documented milestone and decision point.

Separated research track

Consciousness and experiential programs run on their own track with honest readiness levels, so they never carry the core business case.

Risk register

Risks already priced in.

Each major risk has a named mitigation and a line in the budget, including a 20% contingency.

Wildfire

Fire-resistant materials, defensible space and insurance.

Wind loads

Wind-tunnel testing and dome reinforcement.
