Open Questions & Research Agenda

Light City is honest about what is unknown. This page lists the open questions, how we intend to answer them and the stage at which each answer is needed. Questions are tagged with the same horizons used across the library: Proven, Scaling, Emerging, Frontier (see Technology Horizon).

How to contribute

Pick a question, check the bibliography for starting points, and open a GitHub issue with your findings or a proposed study.

Research principles​

  1. Hypotheses are labeled as hypotheses. Visionary material inspires questions. It does not answer them.
  2. Preregister before testing. Methods and outcome measures are published before data is collected.
  3. Publish everything. That includes null and negative results.
  4. Independent partners. Studies of consciousness or health effects are run with or reviewed by outside academic teams.
  5. Consent and privacy first. Participation is voluntary. Physiological and wellbeing data belong to the people who produce them.
