  • Findhorn ecological footprint. 2.71 gha per person against a UK average of 5.4 (Findhorn footprint study) (B).
  • Sieben Linden carbon footprint. About 5.0 t CO₂ per person against a German average of 10.5 t (Sieben Linden) (B). Straw-bale homes (World Habitat) (B).
  • Net-positive building. Kendeda Building, Living Building Challenge (Georgia Tech) (A).
  • Agrivoltaics and pollinators. NREL/NLR (A). USDA Climate Hubs (A).
  • Biodiversity net gain. GOV.UK guidance (A).
  • Night lighting. DarkSky International (A).
  • Bird-safe glass. US Fish and Wildlife Service (A).
  • Stone. UNESCO, dry-stone walling (A). MPA, dimension stone and carbon (B).
