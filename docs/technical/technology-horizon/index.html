Technology Horizon

What exists, what is arriving, and what is still a question, as of November 2026.

Light City should feel like the future without betting the community on hype. Every technology we mention sits on one of four horizons. Documents across the library use these same four tags.

HorizonMeaningLight City ruleTypical TRL
ProvenCommercially mature with long track recordsBuild with it from day one8–9
ScalingIn commercial production now, costs falling fastDesign for it and procure when Stage 5 starts (2028–2031)6–8
EmergingPilot lines or early products, not yet bankableLeave space and interfaces for it, adopt in Phase 24–6
FrontierResearch and hypothesesStudy it openly, never depend on it1–3

The detailed specs, costs and suppliers for each system are in the Technology Catalog.

Scaling — design for it, buy it in Stage 5​

Perovskite-silicon tandem solar​

Tandem cells stack a perovskite layer on silicon to capture more of the spectrum. In 2026 they moved from the lab into factories. LONGi set a 35.5% cell efficiency record, and Tandem PV launched commercial manufacturing in California (pv magazine USA). Heliene and Caelux began commercial production of modules above 27% efficiency in Minnesota, with deliveries in 2027 (pv magazine). GCL won China's first commercial tandem module order (pv magazine). For Light City: a strong candidate for the spire canopy and dome glazing, where every square meter counts. Field durability data from 2027–2028 will decide between tandem and silicon.

Sodium-ion batteries​

Sodium-ion uses abundant materials, stays safe and works well in the cold. CATL's Naxtra reaches 175 Wh/kg, keeps over 90% of its capacity at −40°C and enters mass production by the end of 2026 (CATL). Wood Mackenzie expects cell costs to reach parity with LFP by the end of 2026 (The Driven). For Light City: the preferred stationary storage chemistry for Phase 1 if pricing holds.

Iron-air multi-day storage​

Form Energy's iron-air batteries store about 100 hours of energy using iron, water and air. After the first commercial deployment with Great River Energy, the company is building a 300 MW / 30 GWh system with Xcel Energy and Google (Form Energy). For Light City: the path to riding through cloudy weeks without fossil backup. Most relevant in Phase 2, when loads justify utility-scale units.

Enhanced geothermal​

Fervo Energy's Cape Station in Utah delivered first power in September 2026 and began commercial operation at 33 MW, with roughly 100 MW expected by early 2027 (Fervo Energy; SEC filing). For Light City: site-dependent. If the chosen land has good heat resources, geothermal could provide firm, always-on power and heat from Phase 2. Shallow ground-source heat pumps are proven everywhere.

AI building digital twins​

Reviews of AI and reinforcement-learning building controls report 25–40% HVAC energy savings (MDPI Buildings), with typical operational savings of 15–22% in US studies (Taylor & Francis review). For Light City: the Stage 3 digital model becomes the operating twin of the real city. It tunes comfort, energy and maintenance, and gives residents a visible view of the city's metabolism.

Robotic and 3D-printed construction​

ICON and Lennar completed a 100-home 3D-printed neighborhood at Wolf Ranch, Texas (New Atlas). ICON's newer printer targets multistory walls at much lower cost (SFGate). For Light City: printed foundations, garden walls and studio buildings. Factory-prefabricated CLT panels remain the main method for the domes.

Agrivoltaics​

Placing solar panels over crops can work, but it depends on the climate. A 2026 PNAS analysis found yield losses in humid regions (maize −24%) and gains in semi-arid regions (soy +6%) (PMC). Shade-tolerant vegetables have done well under panels (Michigan State University). For Light City: use it for vegetables and grazing in suitable climates, and match it to the site.

Frontier — study openly, never depend on​

These ideas come from the project's visionary source (The Archetype). We take them seriously as research questions:

  • Group coherence and field effects. Can shared practice measurably synchronize physiology or affect wellbeing beyond the individual? (TRL 1–3)
  • Acoustic and resonance spaces. Do specific frequencies or room geometries improve rest, focus or emotion? (TRL 2–4)
  • Sacred geometry and wellbeing. Do proportion systems such as the golden ratio change how spaces feel and function? (TRL 2–4)
  • Subtle-energy concepts described in the archetype, such as the crystal spire and knowledge transfer. (TRL 1)

Our standard: preregistered studies, independent partners, published results (including null results) and no health claims without evidence. The full list is in Open Questions.

Realism notes — lessons we have already learned​

  • Vertical farming is not the default. Several well-funded vertical farms failed. 80 Acres Farms wound down in 2026, after Bowery and Plenty (Vertical Farm Daily). Fully indoor farms can use several times the energy of greenhouses (Agritecture). Light City grows food in solar greenhouses, food forests and fields, and keeps indoor growing for niche crops such as herbs, microgreens and mushrooms.
  • Timelines slip. Solid-state batteries are years behind early promises. We plan with what ships today and treat new technology as an upgrade path.
  • The spire is the boldest structure. At TRL 4–5 it is a novel assembly of proven parts, so it gets the most engineering review in Stage 2.
