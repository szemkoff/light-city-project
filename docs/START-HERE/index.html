Start Here

Light City is a planned, regenerative community: a city of light, timber and glass, built in livable steps from today's world toward a more conscious future.

The library holds everything in one place, from the vision behind it to the engineering, costs, governance and research. It is written so that a curious newcomer, a future resident, an investor and an engineer can all read the same story and trust the same numbers.

"The city is in the sky until it is in your hands." "To build it on Terra, begin building it in hearts."

Reading paths​

New and curious (15 minutes). Read At a Glance, then Core Concepts, then The Transition.

Thinking of living there. Start with The Transition, then Communospheres, Contribution-Based Residency, Economy and Migration and Governance, and finish on the Residents page.

Investor or partner. Read At a Glance, then Development Stages (financial model), Economy and Migration and Technology Horizon, and finish on the Investors page.

Architect, engineer or builder. Start with Architecture, Beyond the Box and Light on the Land, then Technology Catalog, Energy Systems and the Urban Retrofit Guide.

Researcher. Begin with Open Questions, then Bibliography, and finish with the frontier section of Technology Horizon.

Contributor. Read Contributing, then Quick-Start Guide and Tools & Workflows.

How we hold vision and reality together​

Light City began as a vision that its founder and others describe receiving through meditation and channeled sources. We share that account openly on The Archetype, and we build with engineering discipline. Elsewhere, visionary material is written as "the source describes…". Three conventions run through every page:

  1. Archetype vs. Earth build. Pages say whether they describe the visionary archetype or what we will actually construct.
  2. Four horizons. Every technology is tagged Proven, Scaling, Emerging or Frontier (Technology Horizon). We build with what is proven, design for what is scaling and study what is frontier.
  3. One set of numbers. Every figure matches At a Glance.
  4. Show each image once. Every photo and diagram lives on one page; other pages link to it.
