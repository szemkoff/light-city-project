Light City at a Glance

This page is the single source of truth for the project's numbers. Every other document in the library, along with the website, uses these figures. If you find a number elsewhere that disagrees, this page wins. Please open an issue so we can fix it.

How to read the numbers

Costs and sizes are given as low / target / high scenarios. The target is the planning case used across the site. Everything is a pre-design estimate in 2026 US dollars. Figures will tighten in Stage 2 (Design) and again once a site is selected.

In one paragraph​

Light City is a planned, regenerative community. It starts as a 200-person pilot hamlet of timber-and-glass domes gathered around a solar spire, then grows in phases into a town of 8,000+ residents. It is built with proven technology today, adds emerging technology as it matures, and treats consciousness research as an open, measured inquiry rather than a promise. We are currently finishing Stage 1 (Foundation), and the first residents are targeted for 2031.

Build once, grow outward. Each phase keeps everything built before it: later phases fill reserved plots, add sections to the same spire and add rings outside the existing ones. See Architecture → Phased development & scale.

Land. We are looking for a rural or semi-rural site of 100–500 acres. That is enough for the Phase 1 core, agriculture, buffers and room to grow into Phase 2. The site must sit at least 350 ft (107 m) above sea level. Five candidate regions in four countries (northern New Mexico and southern Oregon in the US, central Portugal, the Granada highlands in Spain and northern Costa Rica) are compared in Site Locations.

Phase 1 is planned as about 110 homes for 200 residents. Fees are per home plus a member fee for each extra person, so break-even is being rebuilt around households (Household Model). Revenue mix: resident fees about 60%, retreats 20%, events and agritourism 10%, licensing and consulting 10%. Target impact return: 8–12% IRR over years 5–10. See Development Stages → Financial Model.

Economy​

The inside economy moves in four phases from dollar fees to Light hours, a time credit where one hour of work earns one credit. A dial sets how much of each fee can be paid in Light hours: 0% in the 2027–2028 pilot, 20–30% at move-in, 50–90% once basics are earned by doing your share. Phase 3, no money inside the community, is an option residents can vote for and review every year. Loan payments, taxes and outside costs always stay in dollars. See Economy and Migration.

Language we use consistently​

  • Archetype describes the image of Light City in the visionary source, at planetary scale (The Archetype). Earth build is what we will actually construct. Documents label which one they mean.
  • Communosphere is a dome neighborhood. Spire is the central light tower. Hamlet → Village → Town are Phases 1–3.
  • Stages (S1–S6) are steps in the project process. Phases (1–3) are physical growth of the city. Stage 5 builds Phase 1.

Full definitions are in the Glossary.
