Glossary

Plain-language definitions of the terms used across the Light City library. Technology terms show their readiness horizon (Proven, Scaling, Emerging, Frontier), as defined in Technology Horizon.

0–9​

3D-printed construction: Building walls and site elements by extruding concrete or earth mixes layer by layer with a robotic printer. Scaling. Light City plans to use it for foundations, garden walls and studios. See Technology Horizon.

A​

Agrivoltaics: Growing crops or grazing animals beneath solar panels. Results depend on climate: yields can fall in humid regions and rise in semi-arid ones. Scaling. See Technology Horizon.

Agroforestry: Growing trees, shrubs and crops together in layered, food-producing systems such as food forests. Proven.

AI building controls: Software that learns how a building behaves and adjusts heating, cooling and lighting automatically, often using reinforcement learning. Reviews report 15–40% energy savings. Scaling. See Technology Horizon.

Archetype: The image of Light City described by the visionary source, at planetary scale with crystal spires and enormous spheres. It is used as inspiration and contrasted with the Earth build. See The Archetype.

B​

Bankable: Proven enough that lenders and insurers will finance it on normal terms. Light City buys Scaling technologies only once they become bankable.

Biodiversity net gain (BNG): Leaving the habitat of a site measurably better than before development, scored with a recognised metric. England has required at least 10% for most new development since February 2024, secured for 30 years. Light City aims for at least 20%. See Light on the Land.

Bird-safe glass: Glass with a visible pattern (fritting, etching or UV markings) spaced no more than about 2 by 4 inches apart, so birds see it as a barrier. Proven.

BIPV: Building-integrated photovoltaics. Solar cells built into glazing, roofs or façades.

C​

CAPEX / OPEX: Capital expenditure (the one-time cost to build) and operating expenditure (the yearly running cost).

CLT: Cross-laminated timber. Large structural panels of glued wood layers. It stores carbon and is the main material for the domes.

Coherence: In our usage, a felt and potentially measurable state of attunement. In a person, it means calm, focused physiology (for example, regular heart-rate variability). In a group, it means trust, shared purpose and synchrony. Group-level effects beyond the social are a frontier research question.

Communosphere: A dome neighborhood of 80–400 residents (80–120 in Phase 1). It combines homes, shared kitchens, gardens and gathering space under a geodesic timber-and-glass shell.

Contribution-based residency: A residency path where work hours offset monthly fees. See Contribution-Based Residency.

D​

DarkSky lighting: Outdoor lighting that is shielded, aimed down, warm in color (3000 K or warmer) and dimmed or switched off when not needed, following DarkSky International. Proven.

Delphin: A city the visionary source describes on another world, and the origin of the spire and communosphere ideas. Inspiration, not a real place. See The Archetype.

Dry-stone walling: Building walls from stones fitted together without mortar. A UNESCO-listed craft found from Ireland to Cyprus and the Azores. See Beyond the Box.

Dial: The share of each home's monthly fee that can be paid in Light hours instead of dollars. It moves up one phase at a time after a measured gate and an 80% resident vote, and steps back automatically if the edge bill is not covered. See Economy and Migration.

Digital twin: A live digital model of the city. It is used first for design and community previews (Stage 3), then to operate buildings and energy efficiently.

E​

Edge bill: The costs Light City must always pay in dollars: loan payments and investor returns, taxes, insurance, outside goods and services, and reserves. See Economy and Migration.

Earth build: What we will physically construct, using standard engineering, permitting and safety practices.

Embodied carbon: The greenhouse gases released to make, transport and build with a material, before a building is used. Local stone, earth and timber are low; cement, steel and glass are high.

EEG: Electroencephalography: measuring the brain's electrical activity through sensors on the scalp. Consumer EEG headbands are Emerging tools for practice and research.

Emerging: The third horizon: pilot lines or early products, not yet bankable (TRL 4–6). Light City leaves room for it and adopts it in Phase 2. See Technology Horizon.

Enhanced geothermal (EGS): Creating heat reservoirs in hot, dry rock by drilling and fracturing it, so geothermal power can work in more places. It provides firm, always-on power. Scaling, and site-dependent. See Technology Horizon.

F​

fNIRS: Functional near-infrared spectroscopy: using light to measure blood-oxygen changes in the brain. Some consumer headbands now combine it with EEG. Emerging.

Faceted curve: A curved form approximated by many flat panels, so standard materials and glazing can be used. See Beyond the Box.

Frontier: The fourth horizon: ideas that are still research questions or hypotheses (TRL 1–3). Light City studies them openly and never depends on them. See Technology Horizon.

Funicular shell: A curved structure shaped so it carries load in pure compression, like an arch or eggshell, allowing very thin sections and much less concrete. Scaling. See Beyond the Box.

G​

Gate: The pass/fail criteria for moving from one stage to the next.

Glulam: Glued laminated timber: wood layers glued into beams and columns. Used alongside CLT in mass timber buildings.

Gridshell: A roof or shell made of thin timber laths laid flat as a grid, bent into a double curve and locked in place. Spans large spaces with little material. Proven. See Beyond the Box.

Ground-source heat pump: A heat pump that exchanges heat with the stable temperature of the ground through buried pipes. Proven and usable almost anywhere. Not the same as enhanced geothermal.

H​

Hamlet → Village → Town: Phases 1, 2 and 3 of physical growth, with 200, 1,500 and 8,000+ residents. See At a Glance.

Heat pump: A machine that moves heat instead of burning fuel to make it. It heats and cools buildings very efficiently. Proven.

Horizon (Proven / Scaling / Emerging / Frontier): Our four-level readiness scale for technologies. See Technology Horizon.

HRV biofeedback: Training slow, regular breathing while watching your heart-rate variability (the natural variation between heartbeats) in real time. It is the best-supported consumer biofeedback method for stress.

I​

Iron-air battery: A battery that stores energy by rusting and un-rusting iron. It is cheap and stores energy for about 100 hours, which helps ride through cloudy weeks. Scaling. See Technology Horizon.

IRR: Internal rate of return. Light City targets an 8–12% impact IRR.

Islanding: A microgrid's ability to disconnect from the utility grid and keep running on its own during outages.

K​

kWp / MWp: Kilowatt-peak and megawatt-peak: the rated output of solar panels under standard full sun. Phase 1 plans 2–5 MWp.

L​

Light hours: Light City's time credit. One hour of work earns one Light hour, whatever the work. Accounts start at zero, carry no interest, have limits, and cannot be bought with or turned into dollars. See Economy and Migration.

LFP battery: Lithium iron phosphate: the safe, long-lived lithium battery chemistry most common in stationary storage today. Proven.

Living Archive: The city's open memory: a spiral gallery in the spire (94–114 m) holding the history of the land and the city, oral histories, live data and a seed library, free for anyone to read and copy. Inspired by the source's living archive. See The Living Archive.

Living machine: A wastewater treatment system that uses plants, microbes and constructed wetlands to clean water biologically. Proven.

Long-duration storage: Energy storage that can deliver power for many hours to days (for example iron-air or flow batteries), rather than the 2–4 hours typical of lithium batteries.

M​

Mass timber: Building with large engineered wood elements such as CLT and glulam. The tallest timber building, Ascent in Milwaukee, is 86.6 m. Proven.

Microgrid: A local energy network (solar, storage, controls) that can run connected to the utility grid or on its own (islanded).

MWh / GWh: Megawatt-hours and gigawatt-hours: amounts of stored or used energy. Phase 1 plans 8–20 MWh of storage.

Moisture buffering: A wall finish's ability to absorb and release humidity, smoothing indoor humidity swings. Clay plaster is especially good at it. See Beyond the Box.

Mycelium materials: Insulation, acoustic panels and packaging grown from fungal roots (mycelium) bonded to agricultural waste. Emerging.

P​

Perovskite-silicon tandem solar: Solar cells that stack a perovskite layer on top of silicon to capture more of the light spectrum, reaching efficiencies above 27% in commercial modules. Scaling. A candidate for the spire canopy and dome glazing. See Technology Horizon.

Phase: A step of physical growth (1–3). Different from a Stage.

Prefabrication: Making building parts (such as CLT panels or dome segments) in a factory, then assembling them on site. It is faster, more precise and less wasteful.

Preregistration: Publishing a study's methods and outcome measures before collecting data, so results cannot be cherry-picked. Required for all Light City wellbeing and consciousness research.

Proven: The first horizon: commercially mature technology with long track records (TRL 8–9). Light City builds with it from day one. See Technology Horizon.

Psilocybin-assisted therapy: Clinical treatment that combines psilocybin with psychotherapy, under review by the US FDA as of November 2026. Light City does not provide or promote psychedelics.

R​

Reinforcement learning: A form of AI that learns by trial and feedback which actions give the best results. Used in AI building controls.

S​

Sacred geometry: Proportion and pattern systems such as the golden ratio and the flower of life, used to plan the city. See Sacred Geometry.

Scaling: The second horizon: in commercial production with costs falling fast (TRL 6–8). Light City designs for it and buys it in Stage 5. See Technology Horizon.

Self-healing concrete: Concrete containing bacteria or capsules that seal small cracks on their own, extending its life. Emerging.

Sociocracy / consent: A governance method based on circles. A decision passes when no one has a reasoned, paramount objection. Light City uses an 80% consent threshold for major decisions. See Governance.

Sodium-ion battery: A battery that uses abundant sodium instead of lithium. It is safe, works well in the cold and is nearing cost parity with LFP. Scaling. It is the preferred Phase 1 storage chemistry if pricing holds. See Technology Horizon.

Solar greenhouse: A greenhouse designed to capture and store the sun's heat, growing food year-round with little added energy. Proven. Light City's main food system, preferred over vertical farms.

Solid-state battery: A battery with a solid electrolyte instead of a liquid one, promising higher energy density and safety. Still pilot-scale in 2026. Emerging, aimed first at vehicles. See Technology Horizon.

Spire (Light Spire): The central tower, the Crystal Point: a twelve-sided faceted glass crystal, 90 m in Phase 1, 150 m in Phase 2 and 240 m in Phase 3, with a wind crown of six turbines in an open lattice under a glass point. It serves as the city's landmark, energy collector and gathering place.

Superadobe (earthbag): Building with long bags filled with moist earth, coiled into domes and vaults. Developed by Nader Khalili at Cal-Earth. Low cost and suited to self-build. Proven, though permits vary. See Beyond the Box.

Stage (S1–S6): A step in the project process: Foundation, Design, Digital, Community, Physical, Operations. Stage 5 builds Phase 1.

T​

Tandem cell: See Perovskite-silicon tandem solar.

The Transition: Our five-movement path from today's life to the Light City future: Awakening, Gathering, Founding, Flourishing, Radiating. See The Transition.

Transparent wood: Wood with its lignin removed and replaced by a clear polymer, making a strong, insulating, translucent panel. Emerging. A possible Phase 2 daylighting material.

TRL: Technology Readiness Level, from 1 (basic idea) to 9 (proven in operation).

V​

Vertical farming: Growing crops indoors in stacked layers under LED lights. The technology works, but energy costs have sunk several large companies. Light City limits it to herbs, microgreens and education. See realism notes.
