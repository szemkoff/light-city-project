# Open Questions & Research Agenda

Light City is honest about what is unknown. This page lists the open questions, how we intend to answer them and the stage at which each answer is needed. Questions are tagged with the same horizons used across the library: **Proven, Scaling, Emerging, Frontier** (see [Technology Horizon](/docs/technical/technology-horizon)).

:::tip How to contribute
Pick a question, check the [bibliography](/docs/research/bibliography) for starting points, and open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/szemkoff/light-city-project/issues) with your findings or a proposed study.
:::

## Research principles

1. **Hypotheses are labeled as hypotheses.** Visionary material inspires questions. It does not answer them.
2. **Preregister before testing.** Methods and outcome measures are published before data is collected.
3. **Publish everything.** That includes null and negative results.
4. **Independent partners.** Studies of consciousness or health effects are run with or reviewed by outside academic teams.
5. **Consent and privacy first.** Participation is voluntary. Physiological and wellbeing data belong to the people who produce them.

## A. Practical questions (answer in Stages 2–4)

| # | Question | Horizon | Method | Needed by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Which site? (climate, water, legal context, cost, access, geothermal potential) | Proven | Weighted site-selection matrix; shortlist visits | S2 (2027) |
| A2 | What spire height and structure are feasible for Phase 1 at the chosen site? | Proven parts / TRL 4–5 assembly | Structural feasibility study with a mass-timber engineer | S2 |
| A3 | Silicon or perovskite-tandem glazing for the spire canopy and domes? | Scaling | Track 2027–2028 field data and warranties; cost per kWh comparison | S5 procurement |
| A4 | LFP or sodium-ion stationary storage? When is long-duration storage justified? | Scaling | Lifecycle cost model; supplier quotes | S5 |
| A5 | What dome diameter and floor count best balance cost, daylight and community size? | Proven | Digital models, daylight and energy simulation | S3 |
| A6 | Which permitting route (planned development, agricultural village, special district)? | Proven | Land-use attorney review per shortlisted site | S2–S4 |
| A7 | What is the right mix of home types and fees for 180-resident break-even? | Proven | Waitlist survey, financial model sensitivity | S4 |

## B. Community and governance (answer in Stage 4)

| # | Question | Method |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | Does the 80% consent model work at 150+ people without stalling? | Use it in the founding circle; track decision times and satisfaction |
| B2 | What trial membership period and exit process best protect both people and community? | Retreat and residency pilots; research on [community durability](https://www.academia.edu/66624398/The_Durability_of_Collective_Action_When_do_Intentional_Communities_Survive_Challenges) |
| B3 | How do we keep the founding spirit alive after the pioneering phase? | Study long-lived communities (Findhorn, Twin Oaks, kibbutzim, Auroville) |
| B4 | Which land-holding structure (community land trust, cooperative, foundation) best secures the long term? | Legal comparison; [research on trust-held communities](https://researchcommons.waikato.ac.nz/server/api/core/bitstreams/fbfac45f-c75e-4963-9c6d-c83c38bd3ff9/content) |
| B5 | How is privacy kept in a highly connected community, including any shared wellbeing data? | Data charter drafted with residents |

## C. Wellbeing and environment (answer in Stages 5–6)

| # | Question | Horizon | Method |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Does living in Light City improve wellbeing compared with residents' previous lives? | Emerging | Longitudinal study (WHO-5, PSS, sleep, HRV) from move-in |
| C2 | Do biophilic, daylight-rich dome interiors measurably improve sleep, mood and focus? | Emerging (TRL 5–7) | Post-occupancy evaluation; comparison across housing types |
| C3 | Does a shared daily practice culture sustain its benefits over years? | Emerging | Cohort follow-up; builds on [meta-analytic evidence](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12984879/) |
| C4 | How much soil carbon and biodiversity does the land regain? | Proven methods | Baseline survey before construction; yearly monitoring |
| C5 | How close does Phase 1 get to energy, water and food self-supply? | Proven / Scaling | Digital twin metering |

## D. Frontier questions (study openly, never depend on)

These come from the project's visionary source ([The Archetype](/docs/concepts/the-archetype)). We restate them as testable questions.

| # | Original idea | Testable question | Horizon |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | The spire holds a "base frequency" | Do particular acoustic or light environments in the spire change measured relaxation or attention? | Frontier (TRL 2–4) |
| D2 | Communospheres amplify coherence | Does group practice inside domes produce more physiological synchrony (HRV, EEG) than the same practice elsewhere? | Frontier (TRL 1–3) |
| D3 | Twelve spheres in a sacred arrangement | Does the plan geometry affect wayfinding, social contact or perceived harmony? | Frontier (TRL 2–4) |
| D4 | "Knowledge transfer" through the field | No current method exists. We keep it as an archived vision question, not active research. | Frontier (TRL 1) |
| D5 | Site "ley lines" and energetic qualities | Can people tell site locations apart in blinded conditions? Otherwise, site selection uses practical criteria only. | Frontier (TRL 1) |
| D6 | Group meditation influences the wider community | Explore existing literature critically; any study is preregistered with independent statisticians | Frontier (TRL 1–3) |
