# Light City at a Glance

This page is the **single source of truth** for the project's numbers. Every other document in the library, along with the website, uses these figures. If you find a number elsewhere that disagrees, this page wins. Please [open an issue](https://github.com/szemkoff/light-city-project/issues) so we can fix it.

:::info How to read the numbers
Costs and sizes are given as **low / target / high** scenarios. The **target** is the planning case used across the site. Everything is a pre-design estimate in 2026 US dollars. Figures will tighten in Stage 2 (Design) and again once a site is selected.
:::

## In one paragraph

Light City is a planned, regenerative community. It starts as a 200-person **pilot hamlet** of timber-and-glass domes gathered around a solar spire, then grows in phases into a town of 8,000+ residents. It is built with proven technology today, adds emerging technology as it matures, and treats consciousness research as an open, measured inquiry rather than a promise. We are currently finishing **Stage 1 (Foundation)**, and the first residents are targeted for 2031.

## Scale by phase

| | Phase 1 — Pilot Hamlet | Phase 2 — Village | Phase 3 — Town |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Residents** | 200 target (150–300) | 1,500 target (1,500–3,000) | 8,000+ (8,000–12,000) |
| **Built core** | 20–30 acres | 150–300 acres | Long-term master plan |
| **Communosphere domes** | 3 domes, 30–45 m | 6–12 domes, 45–60 m | 12+ domes, up to 90 m |
| **Residents per dome** | 80–120 | 120–200 | up to 400 |
| **Spire** | 60–120 m | 120–300 m (staged) | Up to 300 m |
| **Temples** | 1 | 3–5 | Network |
| **Solar** | 2–5 MWp | 5–15 MWp | 15–50 MWp |
| **Storage** | 8–20 MWh | 40–80 MWh | Long-duration mix |
| **Food** | 1–2 ha agroforestry + greenhouses | 5–10 ha, near self-sufficiency | Regional food system |
| **Earliest start** | Construction Dec 2028; residents 2031 | After Phase 1 proves out (mid-2030s) | 2040s+ |

**Build once, grow outward.** Each phase keeps everything built before it: later phases fill reserved plots, add sections to the same spire and add rings outside the existing ones. See [Architecture → Phased development & scale](/docs/design/architecture#phased-development--scale).

**Land.** We are looking for a rural or semi-rural site of **100–500 acres**. That is enough for the Phase 1 core, agriculture, buffers and room to grow into Phase 2. The site must sit at least 350 ft (107 m) above sea level. Five candidate regions in four countries (northern New Mexico and southern Oregon in the US, central Portugal, the Granada highlands in Spain and northern Costa Rica) are compared in [Site Locations](/docs/project/site-locations).

## Signature structures

| Structure | Phase 1 specification | Readiness |
|---|---|---|
| **Communosphere domes** | Geodesic CLT (cross-laminated timber) with steel nodes, solar glazing, 3–5 levels | TRL 7–9 (proven components) |
| **Light Spire** | Steel/CLT hybrid, 60–120 m, 200–500 kWp PV canopy, observation and gathering levels | TRL 4–5 (novel assembly of proven parts) |
| **Temple and plaza** | Timber-frame temple, stone plaza oriented on the sacred-geometry plan | TRL 9 |
| **Microgrid** | Solar + battery storage, grid-tied with islanding | TRL 9 |

TRL means [Technology Readiness Level](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technology_readiness_level), from 1 (idea) to 9 (proven in operation).

## Nature

| Commitment | Target |
|---|---|
| **Land** | At most 15% of the site developed; at least 50% kept as permanent habitat |
| **Biodiversity** | At least 20% net gain over a pre-construction baseline, monitored for 30 years |
| **Energy** | 105% or more from on-site renewables; no fossil fuel; renewable-fuel backup only in long outages |
| **Water** | No net groundwater drawdown; water reused at least once |
| **Night and glass** | DarkSky-compliant outdoor lighting; bird-safe glass on every dome |
| **Footprint** | Residents at or below half the host country's average by year 3 |

Zero impact is not possible; these are the measurable targets. Evidence, limits and all ten commitments: [Light on the Land](/docs/design/light-on-the-land).

### Operations (Phase 1, steady state)

| | Low | **Target** | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| Annual operating cost | $1.8M | **$2.5M** | $4.5M |
| Annual revenue | $2.3M | **$3.5M** | $4.5M |
| Break-even | See note | **Fees ~13% above mid at 90% occupancy** | See note |

Phase 1 is planned as about 110 homes for 200 residents. Fees are per home plus a member fee for each extra person, so break-even is being rebuilt around households ([Household Model](/docs/project/development-stages#household-model-november-2026-draft)). Revenue mix: resident fees about 60%, retreats 20%, events and agritourism 10%, licensing and consulting 10%. Target impact return: **8–12% IRR** over years 5–10. See [Development Stages → Financial Model](/docs/project/development-stages).

## Economy

The inside economy moves in four phases from dollar fees to **Light hours**, a time credit where one hour of work earns one credit. A dial sets how much of each fee can be paid in Light hours: 0% in the 2027–2028 pilot, 20–30% at move-in, 50–90% once basics are earned by doing your share. Phase 3, no money inside the community, is an option residents can vote for and review every year. Loan payments, taxes and outside costs always stay in dollars. See [Economy and Migration](/docs/project/economy-and-migration).

## Homes (monthly, all-inclusive)

| Home | Size | Monthly |
|---|---|---|
| Tiny home | 200–300 sf | $500–700 |
| Studio | 400–500 sf | $700–950 |
| 1-bedroom | 600–800 sf | $950–1,300 |
| 2-bedroom | 900–1,200 sf | $1,300–1,700 |
| 3-bedroom (draft) | 1,300–1,600 sf | $1,700–2,200 |

Prices are per home and cover one adult. Each additional adult pays about $300 a month and each child about $150 (draft). See [who fits where](/homes#fit). The price includes utilities, 10–15 communal meals a month, wellness access, events and maintenance. [Contribution-based residency](/docs/project/contribution-based-residency) lowers fees in exchange for work hours, and pilots run from 2027 to 2028.

## Language we use consistently

- **Archetype** describes the image of Light City in the visionary source, at planetary scale ([The Archetype](/docs/concepts/the-archetype)). **Earth build** is what we will actually construct. Documents label which one they mean.
- **Communosphere** is a dome neighborhood. **Spire** is the central light tower. **Hamlet → Village → Town** are Phases 1–3.
- **Stages (S1–S6)** are steps in the project process. **Phases (1–3)** are physical growth of the city. Stage 5 builds Phase 1.

Full definitions are in the [Glossary](/docs/glossary).
