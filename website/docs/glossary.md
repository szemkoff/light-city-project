# Glossary

Plain-language definitions of the terms used across the Light City library. Technology terms show their readiness horizon (**Proven, Scaling, Emerging, Frontier**), as defined in [Technology Horizon](/docs/technical/technology-horizon).

## 0–9

**3D-printed construction**: Building walls and site elements by extruding concrete or earth mixes layer by layer with a robotic printer. **Scaling.** Light City plans to use it for foundations, garden walls and studios. See [Technology Horizon](/docs/technical/technology-horizon#robotic-and-3d-printed-construction).

## A

**Agrivoltaics**: Growing crops or grazing animals beneath solar panels. Results depend on climate: yields can fall in humid regions and rise in semi-arid ones. **Scaling.** See [Technology Horizon](/docs/technical/technology-horizon#agrivoltaics).

**Agroforestry**: Growing trees, shrubs and crops together in layered, food-producing systems such as food forests. **Proven.**

**AI building controls**: Software that learns how a building behaves and adjusts heating, cooling and lighting automatically, often using **reinforcement learning**. Reviews report 15–40% energy savings. **Scaling.** See [Technology Horizon](/docs/technical/technology-horizon#ai-building-digital-twins).

**Archetype**: The visionary, channeled image of Light City, at planetary scale with crystal spires and enormous spheres. It is used as inspiration and contrasted with the **Earth build**.

## B

**Bankable**: Proven enough that lenders and insurers will finance it on normal terms. Light City buys **Scaling** technologies only once they become bankable.

**BIPV**: Building-integrated photovoltaics. Solar cells built into glazing, roofs or façades.

## C

**CAPEX / OPEX**: Capital expenditure (the one-time cost to build) and operating expenditure (the yearly running cost).

**CLT**: Cross-laminated timber. Large structural panels of glued wood layers. It stores carbon and is the main material for the domes.

**Coherence**: In our usage, a felt and potentially measurable state of attunement. In a person, it means calm, focused physiology (for example, regular heart-rate variability). In a group, it means trust, shared purpose and synchrony. Group-level effects beyond the social are a **frontier** research question.

**Communosphere**: A dome neighborhood of 80–400 residents (80–120 in Phase 1). It combines homes, shared kitchens, gardens and gathering space under a geodesic timber-and-glass shell.

**Contribution-based residency**: A residency path where work hours offset monthly fees. See [Contribution-Based Residency](/docs/project/contribution-based-residency).

## D

**Delphin**: The channeled source of much of the original Light City vision. See [Philosophy](/docs/concepts/philosophy).

**Digital twin**: A live digital model of the city. It is used first for design and community previews (Stage 3), then to operate buildings and energy efficiently.

## E

**Earth build**: What we will physically construct, using standard engineering, permitting and safety practices.

**EEG**: Electroencephalography: measuring the brain's electrical activity through sensors on the scalp. Consumer EEG headbands are **Emerging** tools for practice and research.

**Emerging**: The third horizon: pilot lines or early products, not yet bankable (TRL 4–6). Light City leaves room for it and adopts it in Phase 2. See [Technology Horizon](/docs/technical/technology-horizon#emerging--leave-room-for-it-adopt-in-phase-2).

**Enhanced geothermal (EGS)**: Creating heat reservoirs in hot, dry rock by drilling and fracturing it, so geothermal power can work in more places. It provides firm, always-on power. **Scaling**, and site-dependent. See [Technology Horizon](/docs/technical/technology-horizon#enhanced-geothermal).

## F

**fNIRS**: Functional near-infrared spectroscopy: using light to measure blood-oxygen changes in the brain. Some consumer headbands now combine it with EEG. **Emerging.**

**Frontier**: The fourth horizon: ideas that are still research questions or hypotheses (TRL 1–3). Light City studies them openly and never depends on them. See [Technology Horizon](/docs/technical/technology-horizon#frontier--study-openly-never-depend-on).

## G

**Gate**: The pass/fail criteria for moving from one stage to the next.

**Glulam**: Glued laminated timber: wood layers glued into beams and columns. Used alongside CLT in mass timber buildings.

**Ground-source heat pump**: A heat pump that exchanges heat with the stable temperature of the ground through buried pipes. **Proven** and usable almost anywhere. Not the same as enhanced geothermal.

## H

**Hamlet → Village → Town**: Phases 1, 2 and 3 of physical growth, with 200, 1,500 and 8,000+ residents. See [At a Glance](/docs/at-a-glance).

**Heat pump**: A machine that moves heat instead of burning fuel to make it. It heats and cools buildings very efficiently. **Proven.**

**Horizon (Proven / Scaling / Emerging / Frontier)**: Our four-level readiness scale for technologies. See [Technology Horizon](/docs/technical/technology-horizon).

**HRV biofeedback**: Training slow, regular breathing while watching your heart-rate variability (the natural variation between heartbeats) in real time. It is the best-supported consumer biofeedback method for stress.

## I

**Iron-air battery**: A battery that stores energy by rusting and un-rusting iron. It is cheap and stores energy for about 100 hours, which helps ride through cloudy weeks. **Scaling.** See [Technology Horizon](/docs/technical/technology-horizon#iron-air-multi-day-storage).

**IRR**: Internal rate of return. Light City targets an 8–12% impact IRR.

**Islanding**: A microgrid's ability to disconnect from the utility grid and keep running on its own during outages.

## K

**kWp / MWp**: Kilowatt-peak and megawatt-peak: the rated output of solar panels under standard full sun. Phase 1 plans 2–5 MWp.

## L

**LFP battery**: Lithium iron phosphate: the safe, long-lived lithium battery chemistry most common in stationary storage today. **Proven.**

**Living machine**: A wastewater treatment system that uses plants, microbes and constructed wetlands to clean water biologically. **Proven.**

**Long-duration storage**: Energy storage that can deliver power for many hours to days (for example iron-air or flow batteries), rather than the 2–4 hours typical of lithium batteries.

## M

**Mass timber**: Building with large engineered wood elements such as CLT and glulam. The tallest timber building, Ascent in Milwaukee, is 86.6 m. **Proven.**

**Microgrid**: A local energy network (solar, storage, controls) that can run connected to the utility grid or on its own (islanded).

**MWh / GWh**: Megawatt-hours and gigawatt-hours: amounts of stored or used energy. Phase 1 plans 8–20 MWh of storage.

**Mycelium materials**: Insulation, acoustic panels and packaging grown from fungal roots (mycelium) bonded to agricultural waste. **Emerging.**

## P

**Perovskite-silicon tandem solar**: Solar cells that stack a perovskite layer on top of silicon to capture more of the light spectrum, reaching efficiencies above 27% in commercial modules. **Scaling.** A candidate for the spire canopy and dome glazing. See [Technology Horizon](/docs/technical/technology-horizon#perovskite-silicon-tandem-solar).

**Phase**: A step of physical growth (1–3). Different from a **Stage**.

**Prefabrication**: Making building parts (such as CLT panels or dome segments) in a factory, then assembling them on site. It is faster, more precise and less wasteful.

**Preregistration**: Publishing a study's methods and outcome measures before collecting data, so results cannot be cherry-picked. Required for all Light City wellbeing and consciousness research.

**Proven**: The first horizon: commercially mature technology with long track records (TRL 8–9). Light City builds with it from day one. See [Technology Horizon](/docs/technical/technology-horizon#proven--build-with-it-now).

**Psilocybin-assisted therapy**: Clinical treatment that combines psilocybin with psychotherapy, under review by the US FDA as of November 2026. Light City does not provide or promote psychedelics.

## R

**Reinforcement learning**: A form of AI that learns by trial and feedback which actions give the best results. Used in AI building controls.

## S

**Sacred geometry**: Proportion and pattern systems such as the golden ratio and the flower of life, used to plan the city. See [Sacred Geometry](/docs/concepts/sacred-geometry).

**Scaling**: The second horizon: in commercial production with costs falling fast (TRL 6–8). Light City designs for it and buys it in Stage 5. See [Technology Horizon](/docs/technical/technology-horizon#scaling--design-for-it-buy-it-in-stage-5).

**Self-healing concrete**: Concrete containing bacteria or capsules that seal small cracks on their own, extending its life. **Emerging.**

**Sociocracy / consent**: A governance method based on circles. A decision passes when no one has a reasoned, paramount objection. Light City uses an 80% consent threshold for major decisions. See [Governance](/docs/project/governance).

**Sodium-ion battery**: A battery that uses abundant sodium instead of lithium. It is safe, works well in the cold and is nearing cost parity with LFP. **Scaling.** It is the preferred Phase 1 storage chemistry if pricing holds. See [Technology Horizon](/docs/technical/technology-horizon#sodium-ion-batteries).

**Solar greenhouse**: A greenhouse designed to capture and store the sun's heat, growing food year-round with little added energy. **Proven.** Light City's main food system, preferred over vertical farms.

**Solid-state battery**: A battery with a solid electrolyte instead of a liquid one, promising higher energy density and safety. Still pilot-scale in 2026. **Emerging**, aimed first at vehicles. See [Technology Horizon](/docs/technical/technology-horizon#emerging--leave-room-for-it-adopt-in-phase-2).

**Spire (Light Spire)**: The central tower, 60–120 m in Phase 1, crowned by a solar canopy. It serves as the city's landmark, energy collector and gathering place.

**Stage (S1–S6)**: A step in the project process: Foundation, Design, Digital, Community, Physical, Operations. Stage 5 builds Phase 1.

## T

**Tandem cell**: See **Perovskite-silicon tandem solar**.

**The Transition**: Our five-movement path from today's life to the Light City future: Awakening, Gathering, Founding, Flourishing, Radiating. See [The Transition](/docs/concepts/the-transition).

**Transparent wood**: Wood with its lignin removed and replaced by a clear polymer, making a strong, insulating, translucent panel. **Emerging.** A possible Phase 2 daylighting material.

**TRL**: Technology Readiness Level, from 1 (basic idea) to 9 (proven in operation).

## V

**Vertical farming**: Growing crops indoors in stacked layers under LED lights. The technology works, but energy costs have sunk several large companies. Light City limits it to herbs, microgreens and education. See [realism notes](/docs/technical/technology-horizon#realism-notes--lessons-we-have-already-learned).
