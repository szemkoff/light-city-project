The transition

From one person's attention to a city of light.

The transition happens in five movements. Each one is worthwhile on its own, each is voluntary, and each has a clear way to take part today.

1 · Awakening2 · Gathering3 · Founding4 · Flourishing5 · RadiatingPrinciples

The first technology of Light City is not a building. It is the daily habit of attention.

Five movements

Inner shift and outer form, together.

Movement 1 · Now – 2027 · Stages 1–3

Awakening

From distraction to attention

Every transition begins with a person who notices their own mind: meditation, breathwork, time in nature and honest reflection. Research on mindfulness programs shows solid, modest benefits. That is enough to justify a practice culture, and we never present it as a miracle cure.

Take part now: explore the open library, try a simple daily practice and join the founding circle mailing list.

Outer form
Practice, the library, online circles
Evidence
Small-to-moderate stress reduction
Measure
WHO-5, Perceived Stress Scale
Take part
Read, practice, join the list

Movement 2 · 2027 – 2028 · Stage 4

Gathering

From me to we

About half of intentional communities collapse within two years. The survivors share a common vision and a working way to resolve conflict. So Light City rehearses governance before pouring concrete: seasonal retreats, contribution-based residency pilots and real decisions made together.

Take part: attend a retreat, join the residency pilot or help facilitate circles.

Outer form
Retreats, residency pilots
Gate
Founding circle of 150+
Governance
Circles, 80% consent
Measure
Retention, trust, conflict time
Read more in the docs →

Movement 3 · 2028 – 2031 · Stage 5

Founding

From ideals to daily life

The Pilot Hamlet is built and the first residents move in as buildings open. This is where many communities lose their founding spirit, so organization grows carefully and residents help shape interiors, gardens and rituals.

Take part: invest, build, reserve a home or join a volunteer build season.

Build
3 domes, spire, temple, microgrid
Residents
Join in waves, 200 by 2031
Land
Trust or community land structure
Measure
Performance and cost vs. budget

Movement 4 · 2032+ · Stage 6

Flourishing

From survival to contribution

With housing, food and energy shared, people spend more of their life energy on creativity, care, learning and service. This is the heart of the vision: a culture where consciousness and contribution grow together.

Take part: live here, teach, study or visit as a researcher.

Rhythms
Morning practice, shared meals, festivals
Learning
School, apprenticeships, open courses
Research
Preregistered, published
Revenue
Retreats, licensing, consulting
Read more in the docs →

Movement 5 · 2035+ · Phases 2–3

Radiating

From one place to a pattern

From "our city" to a way of living anyone can adopt. The Village and Town grow from what Phase 1 proves, while open design kits and the Urban Retrofit Guide bring Light City patterns into existing neighborhoods.

Take part: start a practice center, green-roof project or microgrid where you live.

Village
1,500–3,000 residents
Town
8,000+ residents

Principles

What keeps the transition humane.

1

Voluntary at every step

No one is pressured to move to the next movement. Leaving is always respected.

Read more →

2

Each step stands alone

The library, a practice circle and a retreat are worthwhile even if a city is never built.

Read more →

3

Evidence before promises

Claims about health or consciousness follow the research, never the reverse.

Read more →

4

No single guru

Inspiration comes from many sources; authority sits with transparent governance.

Read more →

5

Reversible and adaptive

Every stage has a gate. If it is not met, we pause, learn and adjust.

Read more →

6

Beauty and care

Sacred geometry, light and nature make daily life better, whether or not deeper hypotheses are confirmed.

Read more →
