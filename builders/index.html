For builders

Built from what already works.

Phased construction, geodesic and mass-timber options, acoustic targets and a two-track energy story: a physical microgrid plus optional subtle-environment research.

Systems

Every structure uses materials already standing elsewhere.

From Norway's 85.4 m Mjøstårnet timber tower to commercial geodesic domes, each system carries a technology readiness level (TRL).

Structure · TRL 7–9

Communosphere domes

30–90 m domes in cross-laminated timber and solar glass. Geometry that is both beautiful and proven.

Energy

Solar microgrid

Two-track energy

Grid power and research never share a budget line.

The physical microgrid is specified and costed as engineering. Subtle-environment and resonance studies are optional programs with their own readiness levels.

Track APhysical microgrid: solar, storage and controls, proven at TRL 7–9
Track BResearch environments: acoustics and resonance, exploratory
