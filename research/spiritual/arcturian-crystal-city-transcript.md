## Key points for Light City

- **The crystal spire**: 40,000 ft at the centre of the city; it meets the city's power needs and holds a resonant frequency "for their consciousness".
- **Communospheres**: large spheres in a ring around the city, each holding a town of about 10,000; they keep people "consciously connected", and what one learns, all spheres learn.
- **The living archive**: the Arcturians keep the history of the star seeds in a "living archive" that you can interact with and that "will provide you with the knowledge". The Lemurian Hall of Amenti is described as the same kind of archive.
- **Open knowledge**: the Lemurians were "illuminators" who shared knowledge freely; the Anunnaki and Draconian elites hoarded technology to rule as gods and tried to erase that history.
- **Stewardship of a living planet**: the account of terraforming, the Moon brought in for tides and climate, and the oxygen layer maintained.
- **Welcome without fear**: being "welcomed home" to a wider community means meeting others who look or behave differently without paranoia or fear.
- **Crystal City as harmony**: the Arcturian city is called the "perfect tone of environment" in the universe.
