01 — The vision

Spirit before structure.
Engineering that keeps the promise.

Light City begins as an archetype — a crystal city of light, central spire and gathering spheres — and translates it into something buildable, financeable and governed in the open.

03 — Beyond the box

Curves instead of boxes.
Healthy, simple, affordable.

Curved shells carry load in compression, so they use less material, move air and light more freely and feel calmer inside. We build them from timber, earth and clay — low-tech where it can be, precise where it must be.

Domes · TRL 8–9

Timber gridshell

Bent lattices of thin timber: long spans, little material, warm light.

Homes · TRL 7–9

Earthbag & clay

Superadobe domes of local soil with clay plaster that buffers humidity.

Homes · TRL 6–7

3D-printed earth

Printers shape local soil into curved walls with almost no waste.

Pavilions · TRL 7–8

Thin shells

Funicular vaults a few centimeters thick for markets and gathering halls.
